5. Safety and current validation status

Local-first by default. Keep source media immutable, do not upload it by default, and write generated outputs under artifacts/. Check each CLI's --help before running.
  • Playback generation is separate from video landmark extraction.
  • Use real local inputs and focused artifact checks to establish end-to-end success.
  • PlantUML rendering and automatic diagram consistency checks have not yet been verified.
